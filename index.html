Mental Health Aviation & Clinical Escort Services

Specialist clinical support for people travelling by commercial aircraft

Aero Clinical Services Limited provides Registered Nurse-led clinical escorts for people who need mental-health or healthcare support while travelling — hospital transfers, discharges home, and journeys across New Zealand, Australia and the Pacific.

Get in touch See our services

Services

Each mission is individually assessed before acceptance, with a plan built around the person's clinical needs, risk profile and journey.

Airport-to-airport & door-to-door escort

A qualified clinical escort accompanying the person for the full journey, or airport-to-airport as required.

Hospital & home transfers

Hospital-to-hospital and hospital-to-home transfers, coordinated with the treating and receiving teams.

Domestic, trans-Tasman & international escort

Journeys within New Zealand, to and from Australia, across the Pacific, and internationally by arrangement.

Medication & clinical support

Medication support within the escort's professional scope, plus clinical observation and monitoring throughout.

Therapeutic & risk support

Therapeutic communication and risk monitoring appropriate to the person's mental state and presentation.

Liaison & handover

Coordination with treating teams and airline personnel, and a structured clinical handover at the destination.

Fitness-to-fly decisions and medical clearance remain the responsibility of the person's treating medical team and the airline's own processes. Our role is to provide the agreed clinical escort and support — not to replace those decisions.

Our Clinical Lead

Robert Chanengeta — Registered Nurse
